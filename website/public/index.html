FAQ

Good to Know

Do you use machine translation or AI?

No. Every text is translated by hand, then proofread and adapted to the cultural context of its readers.

Which file types can I upload?

Word (.docx), PDF, plain text, Markdown, HTML and subtitle (.srt) files are counted automatically. You can also upload .doc, .odt, .rtf, .pptx, .xlsx and .epub and enter an estimated word count.

Is my text kept confidential?

Yes. Files are stored privately, only I can access them, and they are permanently deleted after delivery. I’m happy to sign an NDA for larger projects.

What if I want changes?

Revisions are included: two for documents, three for books. Just reply to the delivery email with your notes.

How do I pay?

Payments are processed by Stripe: credit and debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay and other local methods. I never see your card details, and you get a receipt by email.

I have a large or unusual project.

Get in touch for a custom quote. Book series, ongoing work, glossaries and tight deadlines are all welcome.
